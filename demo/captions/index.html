Multi-Language Subtitles & Captions

Supports WebVTT subtitle tracks. Switch between English, Spanish, or Off via the Settings menu or press C.

How Subtitles are Configured

Simply add standard HTML5 <track> elements inside your <video> tag:

<video id="my-video" src="video.mp4">
  <track kind="subtitles" label="English" srclang="en" src="subtitles-en.vtt" default>
  <track kind="subtitles" label="Español" srclang="es" src="subtitles-es.vtt">
</video>
